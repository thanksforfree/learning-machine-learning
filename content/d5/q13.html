Text samajh liya. Awaaz mein sequence model ka input kya hota hai? Raw wave?

Raw wave use ho sakti hai, par beginner intuition ke liye spectrogram best hai. Awaaz ko time ke saath frequency-energy ki image bana do.

Matlab “hello” ek picture ban gaya?

Haan. Jaise music app mein equalizer hilta hai, waise hi. Model image jaisa pattern padhta hai, phir characters/words nikalta hai.

Awaaz se spectrogram

Microphone pressure values record karta hai: ek lambi wave. Spectrogram us wave ko chhote time windows mein todta hai aur har window mein kaunsi frequencies strong hain, ye dikhata hai. X-axis time, Y-axis frequency, color energy.

Phonemes wala purana route vs end-to-end

Purane speech systems aksar pipeline banate the: audio se phonemes, phonemes se words, words se sentence. Phoneme matlab sound unit, jaise “ka”, “aa”, “t” type building blocks. Pipeline understandable thi, par har stage ki galti next stage ko pass ho jaati thi.

End-to-end model bolta hai: spectrogram do, directly characters/words predict karunga. Ye simple sounding hai, par data bahut chahiye. Hazaaron ghante audio aur transcripts ke bina model accent, noise, speed, mic quality sab nahi seekh paata.

Old pipelineAudio → phonemes → words. Debug easy, engineering heavy.
End-to-endAudio → text. Data hungry, par large datasets pe strong.

Speech ke liye attention model

Speech input frames text output se bahut zyada hote hain. Ek second audio mein 100 frames ho sakte hain, par output kuch characters. Attention decoder ko relevant audio frames pe focus karne deta hai. “chai” likhte waqt model us sound ke frames pe dhyan de sakta hai.

P(text | audio) = Π⟨t⟩ P(char⟨t⟩ | previous chars, audio features)
  • audio features = spectrogram frames ya encoder outputs
  • previous chars = decoder already kya likh chuka

CTC: lambi awaaz ko chhote text se align karna

Speech mein exact alignment mushkil hai. “chai” ke kis millisecond pe “ch” khatam aur “ai” start? CTC ek clever shortcut deta hai: har audio frame pe character ya blank predict karo. Baad mein lagataar repeats collapse karo, phir blanks hatao. Beech ka blank do same letters ko alag rakhta hai, jaise n _ n se “nn”.

CTC collapse: repeat characters ko merge karo, phir blank “_” hatao
  • Blank = “is frame pe naya character nahi”
  • Repeats = same sound several frames tak stretch ho sakta hai
Frame outputsAfter repeat collapseAfter removing blanks
c c _ h h a _ i ic _ h a _ ichai
n _ n a an _ n anna

Second row important hai. Blank beech mein ho to repeated letters separate reh sakte hain. Isliye “nna” ban sakta hai, sirf “na” nahi.

def ctc_collapse(path):
    out = []
    for ch in path:
        if not out or ch != out[-1]:
            out.append(ch)
    return "".join(ch for ch in out if ch != "_")  # blanks hatao

print(ctc_collapse(["c", "c", "_", "h", "h", "a", "_", "i", "i"]))

Trigger word detection: “Hey phone” sunte hi label 1

Ab task alag hai: continuous audio aa raha hai, model ko batana hai trigger word abhi bola gaya ya nahi. Har time frame pe label output karo: 0 normally, 1 trigger word ke baad.

Audio stream→RNN/Conv model→0 0 0 1 1 1

Problem: labels imbalanced hote hain. 10 seconds audio mein “hey phone” sirf 0.5 second hai, baaki sab 0. Agar sirf ek frame pe 1 lagaya, model mostly zeros predict karke bhi happy ho sakta hai. Practical trick: trigger word ke baad 1 ko thodi der tak rakho, jaise 50 frames. Signal clearer ho jaata hai.

SetupFramesPositive labelsPositive ratio
Single-frame 1100010.1%
1 ko 50 frames tak rakha1000505%

5% bhi imbalanced hai, par 0.1% se better. Model ko “haan, yahan kuch hua” ka zyada training signal milta hai.

Dhyan rakhna: Speech demos quiet room mein impressive lagte hain. Real duniya mein fan noise, traffic, accent, cheap mic, overlapping speakers — sab test karo.
Yaad rakhna: Speech model audio frames ko text ya trigger labels se jodta hai; CTC alignment ko simple banata hai, trigger detection imbalance ko carefully handle karta hai.

Dimaag mein aaye sawaal

Spectrogram image hai, to CNN enough nahi?

CNN useful ho sakta hai, par speech mein time order bhi important hai. Isliye CNN + RNN, attention, ya transformer style models use hote hain.

CTC mein blank space character jaisa hai?

Nahi. Blank special “no character now” symbol hai. Space actual text ka character ho sakta hai, blank alignment helper hai.

Trigger word ke baad 1 kyu, word ke dauran kyu nahi?

Wake word complete hone ke baad hi sure signal milta hai. “hey pho...” sunke jaldi fire karoge to false alarm badh sakta hai.

CTC path “a a _ b b _” collapse hone ke baad final text kya?

Repeats collapse: “a _ b _”. Blanks hatao: ab.

2000 frames mein trigger ke baad 80 frames ko 1 rakha. Positive ratio kya?

80 / 2000 = 0.04 = 4%.

End-to-end speech ko hazaaron ghante data kyu chahiye?

Kyuki model ko accents, background noise, speaking speed, pronunciation, aur spelling mapping sab examples se hi seekhna padta hai.
