Dost, computer vision ka naam sunte hi mujhe lagta hai ki photo dekhna to insaan wali cheez hai. Computer kya dekhega?

Computer ko photo bas numbers ki badi Excel sheet dikhti hai. Har pixel mein red, green, blue ki value. Par haan, us sheet mein pattern chhupa hota hai.

Pattern matlab?

Jaise chai ke cup ka kinara, traffic signal ka gol shape, shirt ki sleeve ki line. CNN pehle aise edges pakadna seekhta hai, phir un edges se parts, phir poora object.

Aur normal neural network se ye kaam nahi ho sakta?

Ho sakta hai chhoti photo pe. Par badi photo aate hi parameters ka tiffin dabba phat jaata hai. Isi liye convolution wali soch chahiye.

Computer vision ke teen common kaam

ClassificationPhoto mein kya hai? Jaise “ye mango hai ya apple?” Sirf label.
DetectionKya hai aur kahan hai? Jaise CCTV frame mein helmet kis box mein hai.
Style transferContent ek photo ka, style doosri image ka. Jaise tumhari selfie ko rangoli-jaisi texture dena.

In teeno mein first useful clue aksar edge hota hai. Agar object ka boundary hi nahi dikha, to aage ki pehchaan mushkil ho jaati hai.

Badi photo ki asli dikkat

Phone se li hui 1200 × 800 RGB photo socho. Har pixel ke 3 channels hote hain.

inputs = height × width × channels = 1200 × 800 × 3 = 28,80,000
  • height, width = photo ke pixel rows aur columns
  • channels = RGB mein 3: red, green, blue
  • 28.8 lakh inputs = sirf ek photo ka raw vector

Agar first hidden layer mein 800 neurons rakh diye, to weights lagbhag 28.8 lakh × 800 = 230.4 crore. Arre bhai, pehli layer hi full IPL auction ban gayi. CNN ka idea hai: chhoti khidki se local pattern dekho, aur wohi khidki poori photo pe ghumao.

Sobel, Scharr, aur learned filters

Purane computer vision mein humans filters design karte the. Sobel jaisa filter center ke aas-paas edge ko thoda zyada weight deta hai. Scharr bhi edge detection ke liye ek hand-made filter family hai.

CNN mein twist ye hai: hum filter ke 9 numbers manually nahi bharte. Training ke dauraan network khud decide karta hai ki kaunsa filter useful hai — koi vertical edge, koi texture, koi curve, koi shirt ka button.

import numpy as np
patch = np.array([[2, 2, 9],
                  [2, 2, 9],
                  [2, 2, 9]])
f = np.array([[1, 0, -1],
              [1, 0, -1],
              [1, 0, -1]])
score = np.sum(patch * f)  # -21, strong vertical edge
Dhyan rakhna: Edge detector ka output “object mil gaya” nahi hota. Ye sirf low-level clue hai. CNN mein bahut layers milke simple edges se complex shapes banati hain.
Yaad rakhna: Photo numbers ki grid hai; filter chhoti torch hai; edge map batata hai ki badlaav kahan strong hai.

Dimaag mein aaye sawaal

Output 5×5 kyu aaya, 7×7 kyu nahi?

3×3 filter bina padding ke 7×7 photo mein sirf 5 positions vertically aur 5 positions horizontally fit hota hai. Formula next page mein detail se aayega.

Negative edge bhi useful hai?

Haan. Sign direction batata hai. Bright-left dark-right aur dark-left bright-right dono edge hain, bas opposite direction.

Filter ke numbers random se start hote hain?

Training ke start mein random hote hain. Backprop unhe dheere-dheere useful pattern detectors bana deta hai.
