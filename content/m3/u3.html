K-means ka bhi cost function hota hai? Matlab clustering mein bhi marks milte hain?

Haan! Har point apne center se kitna door hai — un dooriyon ke square ka average. Isko distortion kehte hain. Achhe groups ka simple sign: point apne center ke paas, distortion kam.

J = (1/m) × Σ ||point − uska center||²
  • ||point − center||² = har feature ka farak square karke jodo — yani squared Euclidean distance.
  • Step 2 (points ko paas wale center pe bhejna) → J kam hota hai, kyunki har point ne sabse kam doori wala center chuna.
  • Step 3 (center ko average pe le jaana) → J kam hota hai, kyunki average hi woh jagah hai jahan squares ka total sabse kam hota hai.
  • Isliye J har round ya to ghatega ya same rahega — kabhi nahi badhega. Badhe to code mein bug hai!

Pichle page ka example yaad hai? Aakhri groups {1,2,3} center 2 aur {10,11,12} center 11. Dooriyon ke square: 1, 0, 1, 1, 0, 1 → total 4 → J = 4/6 ≈ 0.67. Chhota number, matlab groups tight.

K kitna rakhein?

Elbow methodK = 1, 2, 3 … ke liye J plot karo. J hamesha ghatega, par ek jagah ghatna achanak dheema hota hai — graph mein “kohni” (elbow). Wahan ka K. Par aksar kohni saaf dikhti hi nahi.
Kaam ke hisaab se (better)Groups kis kaam aayenge? T-shirt ke liye K = 3 (S, M, L) sasta banega; K = 5 (XS–XL) better fit par zyada kharcha. Business trade-off se chuno.
Galti mat karna: Sirf “sabse kam J” dekh ke K mat chuno. K badhaoge to J hamesha kam hoga — har point ka apna group ho to J = 0, par woh bekaar hai.

Dimaag mein aaye sawaal

Har baar alag result kyu aata hai?

Random shuruaat ki wajah se. Kai baar chala ke best J wala lo — ya code mein random “seed” fix karo taaki result dobara same aaye.

Kya K-means ke alawa bhi clustering methods hain?

Haan — DBSCAN, hierarchical clustering, Gaussian mixtures aur bhi options hain. K-means sabse simple aur popular shuruaat hai, isliye pehle ise pakad lo.

K badhaane se J hamesha kyu ghatta hai?

Zyada centers matlab points ko paas center milne ka chance zyada. Extreme case: har point apna center, J = 0. Isliye J alone se K choose nahi karte.

K-means non-spherical groups pe kaisa hai?

Kamzor. K-means round-ish clusters pasand karta hai. Moon-shape, uneven density, ya weird shapes ho to DBSCAN/hierarchical better ho sakte hain.
