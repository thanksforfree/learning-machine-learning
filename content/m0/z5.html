Average to pata hai. Par ye variance, standard deviation, probability — ye kya hai?

Teen dost ki kahani sunao. Class ke do groups ne test diya. Dono ka average 50 marks. Kya dono same hain?

Average same hai to same hi honge?

Nahi! Group A: 48, 50, 52 — sab average ke paas. Group B: 10, 50, 90 — bahut phaile hue. Average sirf “beech” batata hai. Kitna phaila hai — woh variance aur standard deviation batate hain.

To average kabhi-kabhi aadhi kahani batata hai?

Exactly. Average news headline hai; variance andar ki report. Dono dekho, tab picture clear hoti hai.

Mean (average) = μ

μ = (sab numbers ka total) ÷ (kitne numbers)
  • [48, 50, 52] → 150 ÷ 3 = 50
  • [10, 50, 90] → 150 ÷ 3 = 50

Variance aur standard deviation (σ) — step by step

StepGroup A: 48, 50, 52Group B: 10, 50, 90
1. Har number − average−2, 0, 2−40, 0, 40
2. Square karo4, 0, 41600, 0, 1600
3. Average = variance (σ²)8 ÷ 3 ≈ 2.73200 ÷ 3 ≈ 1067
4. Root = std deviation (σ)≈ 1.6≈ 32.7

σ ka matlab: “aam taur pe ek number average se kitna door hota hai”. Group A mein ~1.6 marks, Group B mein ~33 marks. Square isliye kiya taaki −40 aur +40 cancel na hon — Module 1 ke cost function jaisa hi trick!

Bell curve (normal / Gaussian distribution)

Bahut saari cheezein — logon ki height, machine ka temperature — ek ghante (bell) jaisi shape banati hain: zyadatar values average ke paas, door wali kam.

Do independent cheezein ek saath: chances multiply karo. Do sikke dono heads: 0.5 × 0.5 = 0.25. (Anomaly detection mein har feature ka chance aise hi multiply hota hai.)

“Independent” ka matlab: ek ka result doosre ko influence nahi kar raha. Jaise do alag coins. Par India match jeeta aur WhatsApp pe memes badhe — woh independent nahi 😄.

Ek hi cheez ke saare options ka total = 1. Dice ke 6 options × 1/6 = 1. (Softmax yahi guarantee karta hai.)

Yaad rakhna: μ = beech kahan, σ = kitna phaila, probability = kitna chance (0 se 1).

Dimaag mein aaye sawaal

Variance aur σ dono kyu? Ek kaafi nahi?

Variance ki unit square ho jaati hai (marks²), jo ajeeb hai. σ = root(variance) wapas original unit (marks) mein aata hai — samajhna aasan. Kaam dono ka same: phailaav naapna.

Model 0.8 probability bole to kya 10 mein 8 baar sahi?

Achhe se calibrated model ho to haan, aise 10 cases mein lagbhag 8 sach mein positive hone chahiye. Par har model calibrated nahi hota — isliye check karna padta hai.

ML mein ye kahan aayega?

Feature scaling (μ, σ), anomaly detection (bell curve), logistic regression (probability), softmax (total = 1), reinforcement learning (random outcomes ka average).
