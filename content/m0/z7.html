Derivative / calculus ka naam sunke hi bhaagne ka mann karta hai.

Ek sawaal: tum bike pe ho aur speedometer 40 dikha raha hai. Iska matlab?

Us pal main 40 km per ghanta ki speed se chal raha hoon.

Mubarak ho, tumne derivative samajh liya! Derivative = us pal pe cheez kitni tezi se badal rahi hai. Speed = distance ka derivative. Bas yahi hai.

Sach mein? Itna hi?

Intuition ke level pe haan. Formal maths baad mein aa sakti hai; pehle feel pakdo. Speedometer wali feel hi ML mein slope ban ke aati hai.

Curve ka slope har jagah alag

Seedhi line ka slope har jagah same. Par katori (y = x²) ka? Kahin tedhi, kahin flat. Kisi ek point pe curve ko chhoone wali chhoti si seedhi line ka slope = us point pe derivative.

Derivative nikaalne ka “hila ke dekho” tareeka

Formula yaad nahi? Koi baat nahi. Input ko thoda sa badlo aur dekho output kitna badla.

y = x², point x = 3Hisaab
x = 3y = 9
x = 3.001 (0.001 badhaya)y = 9.006001
y kitna badla ÷ x kitna badla0.006 ÷ 0.001 ≈ 6

To x = 3 pe slope ≈ 6. (Formula se: y = x² ka derivative = 2x = 2 × 3 = 6. Same!) Aise hi dJ/dw padho: “w thoda badhaaun to cost J kitna badlegi?”

Yahan numbers khud check karo: 9.006001 − 9 = 0.006001. 0.001 se divide karoge to lagbhag 6. Formula koi jaadu nahi kar raha.

ML ka connection: Cost katori ka sabse neecha point wahan hai jahan slope 0 hai. Gradient descent slope dekh ke ulti disha mein chalta hai: slope + ho to peeche, slope − ho to aage. Jab slope ~0, pahunch gaye.

Chain rule — domino effect

Petrol ka daam badha → auto ka kiraya badha → tumhara mahine ka kharcha badha. Agar petrol ₹1 badhne se kiraya ₹0.5 badhta hai, aur kiraya ₹1 badhne se kharcha ₹30 badhta hai — to petrol ₹1 badhne pe kharcha? 0.5 × 30 = ₹15. Chain ke har link ka asar multiply karo. Yahi chain rule hai, aur yahi backpropagation ka dil hai.

Petrol×0.5→Kiraya×30→Kharcha: 0.5 × 30 = 15

Partial derivative (∂): jab kai inputs ho (w aur b), to ek ko thoda hilao, baaki ko fixed rakho. ∂J/∂w = “sirf w hilaya to J kitna badla”. Gradient = saare partial derivatives ki list.

Yaad rakhna: Derivative = “thoda hilaaun to kitna badlega?”. Minimum pe ye 0. Chain rule = chain ke asar multiply karo.

Dimaag mein aaye sawaal

d/dx, ∂, ∇ — ye sab kya hai?

d/dx = “x ke hisaab se derivative”. ∂ = wahi, jab kai variables ho (partial). ∇ (nabla) = gradient, yaani saare partials ki list. Teeno ka sawaal same: “thoda hilaaun to kitna badlega?”

Kya mujhe derivative ke formulas practice karne padenge?

Is course ke liye nahi. TensorFlow automatic nikaalta hai. Tumhe bas intuition chahiye: slope ka sign direction batata hai, size batata hai kitni tezi.

Kya hamesha minimum hi dhundhte hain?

ML mein aam taur pe cost ka minimum. Kabhi kisi cheez ka maximum (jaise reward) — tab ulti disha mein chalo (gradient ascent).
