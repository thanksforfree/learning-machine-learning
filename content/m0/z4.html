Power kitni tezi se badhti hai — ye mehsoos karo

Ek kahani: tumhe roz paisa double milega, pehle din ₹1. Din 10 pe ₹512. Din 20 pe ₹5 lakh+. Din 30 pe ₹53 crore+! Isko exponential growth kehte hain — shuru mein dheere, phir rocket.

Sigmoid ab samajh aayega

sigmoid(z) = 1 / (1 + e⁻ᶻ)
  • z = 4: e⁻⁴ = 0.018 → 1 / 1.018 = 0.98
  • z = 0: e⁰ = 1 → 1 / 2 = 0.5
  • z = −4: e⁴ = 54.6 → 1 / 55.6 = 0.018
  • Neeche hamesha 1 + (positive number) → answer kabhi 1 se zyada nahi, kabhi 0 se kam nahi. Isliye probability ke liye perfect!

log — power ka ulta sawaal

Aur log?

Power puchhta hai: “2 ko 3 baar multiply karo to kya aayega?” → 8. Log ulta puchhta hai: “8 banane ke liye 2 ko kitni baar multiply karna padega?” → 3. Bas. log₂(8) = 3.

ML mein aam taur pe natural log (ln ya log) use hota hai jo e ke base pe hai. Tumhe shape yaad rakhni hai:

Yaad rakhna: Power = baar-baar multiply. ez = hamesha positive growth. log = ulta sawaal; chhote chance pe −log bada ho jaata hai.

Dimaag mein aaye sawaal

Calculator mein e aur log kaise?

Python: np.exp(2) = 7.39, np.log(0.5) = −0.69. Haath se kabhi calculate nahi karna padega.

log(0) kya hai?

Defined nahi — minus infinity ki taraf jaata hai. Isliye code mein kabhi-kabhi chhota sa number (1e-15) jod dete hain taaki log(0) na ho. TensorFlow ye khud sambhalta hai.

log se multiply wala kaam jodna ban jaata hai — ye kya hai?

Ek property: log(a × b) = log(a) + log(b). Bahut chhote numbers ko multiply karne ki jagah unke log jodna computer ke liye zyada safe hai. Abhi bas jaan lo, zaroorat pe kaam aayega.
