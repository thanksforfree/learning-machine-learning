Chalo ghar ka price predict karte hain. Shuru kahan se karein?

Pehle data ko dekho. Ek graph banao — neeche (x-axis) ghar ka size, upar (y-axis) uska price. Har purana bika hua ghar graph pe ek dot ban jayega.

Theek hai, dots aa gaye. Ab?

Dekho dots upar ki taraf ja rahe hain — bada ghar, zyada price. Ab ek seedhi line kheencho jo dots ke beech se aise guzre ki sabke kareeb rahe. Bas! Yahi line tumhara model hai.

Aur naye ghar ka price kaise nikalein?

Naya ghar 1600 sq ft ka hai? Neeche 1600 pe jao, seedha upar line tak, aur wahan se left dekho. Jo value mile, wahi predicted price. Computer yahi kaam ek chhote formula se karta hai.

Line thodi galat hogi to?

Hogi hi. Property market mein broker, location, negotiation — sab noise hai. Hum perfect bhavishyavaani nahi, useful andaaza bana rahe hain.

f(x) = w · x + b
  • x = ghar ka size (input)
  • w = “weight” ya slope → har 1 sq ft badhne pe price kitna badhta hai
  • b = “bias” ya starting value → line kis height se shuru hoti hai
  • f(x) = predicted price (yahi ŷ hai)

Numbers se dekho

Maan lo training ke baad model ne seekha: w = 0.04 (yaani ₹4,000 per sq ft) aur b = 10 lakh.

SizeCalculationPredicted price
800 sq ft0.04 × 800 + 1042 lakh
1200 sq ft0.04 × 1200 + 1058 lakh
1600 sq ft0.04 × 1600 + 1074 lakh

Dekha? Har 400 sq ft badhne pe price 16 lakh badh raha hai (400 × 0.04). Yahi w ka matlab hai — size ka “per-unit asar”.

w badlo to?w bada → line zyada tedhi (size ka asar zyada). w = 0 → line bilkul flat (size ka koi asar nahi). w negative → line neeche jaati hai.
b badlo to?Poori line upar ya neeche khisak jaati hai, tedhapan wahi rehta hai.

Line ko haath se padhna

Zero-background rule: line ka output blind trust mat karo, ek-do numbers daal ke feel lo. Maan lo x = 1000 sq ft.

LineHisaabFeeling
0.04x + 100.04 × 1000 + 10 = 50Base model
0.06x + 100.06 × 1000 + 10 = 70w bada, size ka asar zyada
0.04x + 200.04 × 1000 + 20 = 60b bada, poori line upar

Yahan se intuition pakdo: w line ko ghumata hai, b line ko upar-neeche khiskata hai. Dono milke final price banate hain.

Yaad rakhna: Linear regression = data ke beech se best seedhi line, jo w (tedhapan) aur b (shuruaat) se banti hai.

Dimaag mein aaye sawaal

Line hi kyu? Curve kyu nahi?

Hamesha simple se shuru karo — samajhna aasan, galti pakadna aasan. Aur haan, curve bhi bana sakte ho, usi trick se jo topic 07 mein hai.

w aur b ki value kaun decide karta hai — main?

Nahi! Yahi to magic hai. Tum data doge, aur algorithm (gradient descent, topic 04) khud best w aur b dhundhega. Tumhara kaam hai sahi data dena, sahi type ka model chunna, aur result check karna.

b ko “bias” kyu kehte hain? Kya model biased hai?

Nahi, yahan “bias” bas starting value ka naam hai. Module 2 mein “high bias” ek alag concept (underfitting) ke liye aayega — dono ko mix mat karna.

“Univariate linear regression” sunke dar lagta hai

Uni = ek, variate = variable. Matlab bas ek input (size) wali line. Itna hi. Bade naam, chhota kaam.

Kya ye bilkul sahi price batayega?

Nahi. Asli prices pe location, mood, negotiation sab ka asar hota hai — isko “noise” kehte hain. Model ek achha andaaza deta hai, bhavishyavaani nahi.
