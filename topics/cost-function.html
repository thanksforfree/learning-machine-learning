Par dots ke beech se to 100 alag lines kheench sakte hain. Best kaunsi?

Ekdum sahi sawaal! Jaise teacher answer sheet check karta hai, waise hi har line ko check karo. Har ghar ke liye dekho: line ne kya bola vs asli price kya tha. Jo farak hai, woh galti (error) hai.

Phir saari galtiyaan jod dein?

Almost. Ek chhota twist hai: pehle har galti ka square karo, phir sab ka average lo. Jo number aaye, woh us line ka “cost” hai. Jitna kam cost, utni achhi line.

Cost matlab report card ka marks?

Haan, bas ulta report card: kam score = achha. Model ka goal hai is score ko neeche kheenchna.

Do lines ka muqabla

Teen ghar hain: (1000 sq ft, 50 lakh), (1500, 80 lakh), (2000, 95 lakh).

Cost J = total ÷ (2 × 3)8.3 ✓ jeet gayi75
J(w, b) = (1 / 2m) × Σ ( f(x⁽ⁱ⁾) − y⁽ⁱ⁾ )²
  • f(x⁽ⁱ⁾) − y⁽ⁱ⁾ = i-th ghar pe galti (guess − asli)
  • ( )² = galti ka square
  • Σ (sigma) = “sab ghar ke liye jodo”
  • 1/m = average lena
  • 1/2 = bas calculation neat karne ke liye (aage 2 cancel ho jata hai). Best line isse nahi badalti.

Yahan ek baar table ke numbers khud jodo: Line A ka total 50 hai, phir 6 se divide. Cost 8.33. Bas, formula table ka compact version hai.

Katori (bowl) wali picture — ye sabse important hai

Har possible w ke liye cost nikaalo aur graph banao. Ek katori jaisi shape banti hai. Bahut chhota w → line bahut flat → galti zyada. Bahut bada w → line bahut tedhi → phir galti zyada. Beech mein ek point pe cost sabse kam — wahi best w hai.

w aur b dono ho to ye 3D katori ban jaati hai. Usko upar se dekho to same-height wale points chhalle (rings) banate hain — jaise map pe pahad ke contour. Isko contour plot kehte hain. Sabse andar wala chhota chhalla = best (w, b).

Yaad rakhna: Cost function line ki “galti ka score” hai. Training ka poora goal = ye score kam se kam karna.

Dimaag mein aaye sawaal

Square kyu? Seedha galti kyu nahi jodte?

Line A dekho: galti −5 aur +5. Seedha jodo to 0 — lagega line perfect hai! Square karne se minus gayab ho jata hai. Bonus: badi galti ko zyada saza milti hai. Galti 2 → 4, par galti 10 → 100.

Cost 0 ho gaya to model perfect hai?

Training data pe perfect. Par ho sakta hai model ne data ratt liya ho aur naye ghar pe fail ho jaye. Isko overfitting kehte hain — topic 11.

Loss aur cost mein kya farak hai?

Loss = ek example ki galti. Cost = saare examples ki loss ka average. Ek ghar vs poori colony.

1/2 wala funda abhi bhi samajh nahi aaya

Tension mat lo. Kisi bhi cheez ko 2 se divide karo to jo line pehle sabse kam cost wali thi, woh ab bhi sabse kam cost wali rahegi. Ranking nahi badalti. Bas aage slope nikaalte waqt formula saaf dikhta hai.

✍️ Ab tumhari baari (pehle khud socho, phir answer kholna)

Guesses [10, 20], asli [12, 20]. Cost J (1/2m wala)?

Galti −2, 0 → square 4, 0 → total 4 → 4 ÷ (2 × 2) = 1.

Cost bahut kam hai — iska kya matlab?

Line training data ke bahut kareeb hai. (Naye data pe bhi achhi hai ya nahi, woh alag check.)
