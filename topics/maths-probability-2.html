Probability Level 1 mein chance tha. Level 2 mein kya naya hai?

Ab chance akela nahi, context ke saath aayega. “Baarish ka chance” alag, “cloudy hai to baarish ka chance” alag.

Language model bhi aise hi sochta hai?

Haan. “chai” ke baad “peena” ka chance, “train” ke baad “late” ka chance — pichhle shabdon ke condition pe.

Conditional probability: P(A | B)

P(A | B) padho: “B ho chuka hai, ab A ka chance kya?” Example: 100 din observe kiye. 30 din cloudy the. Un 30 cloudy dinon mein 18 din baarish hui.

SawaalHisaabAnswer
Cloudy ka chance30 ÷ 1000.30
Baarish | Cloudy18 ÷ 300.60
Cloudy aur baarish dono0.30 × 0.600.18
P(A and B) = P(B) × P(A | B)
  • P(B) = pehle context ka chance.
  • P(A | B) = context milne ke baad A ka chance.

Sentence probability = chain rule

Sentence ko left se right banao. Har shabd ka chance pichhle shabdon pe depend karega.

P(y₁, y₂, y₃) = P(y₁) × P(y₂ | y₁) × P(y₃ | y₁,y₂)
  • “main” ka chance maan lo 0.8.
  • “main” ke baad “chai” ka chance 0.5.
  • “main chai” ke baad “peeta” ka chance 0.25.
  • Sentence chance = 0.8 × 0.5 × 0.25 = 0.1.

Likelihood — data dekh ke model ko score dena

Likelihood ka mood: “Agar model ye bol raha hai, to hamara observed data kitna believable hai?” Coin example: 10 toss mein 8 heads, 2 tails aaye. Heads ka chance p ho to tails ka 1 − p; har toss ka chance multiply karke poore observed result ka likelihood milta hai.

ModelLikelihood for 8H, 2TValue
Coin heads chance 0.70.7⁸ × (1 − 0.7)² = 0.7⁸ × 0.3²0.00519
Coin heads chance 0.50.5⁸ × (1 − 0.5)² = 0.5¹⁰0.00098

0.7 wala model is data ko zyada believable banata hai. Maximum likelihood training ka idea: parameters aise choose karo jo training labels ko high probability dein.

Negative log-likelihood = cross-entropy loss

Products chhote hote jaate hain, isliye log use karte hain. High probability ko chhoti saza, low probability ko badi saza.

Correct class ko probabilityLoss = −log(p)Feel
0.70.357Achha, chhoti saza
0.21.609Kaafi galat
0.12.303Confident galti, badi saza

Upar sentence example ka log loss: −(log 0.8 + log 0.5 + log 0.25) = 2.303, kyunki total probability 0.1 thi aur −log(0.1) = 2.303.

argmax vs sampling

Softmax model har option ka probability distribution deta hai. Example scores [2, 1, 0.1] ka softmax ≈ [0.659, 0.242, 0.099].

argmaxSabse bada probability choose karo. Predictable, safe. Yahan class 1 (0.659) choose hogi.
samplingProbability ke hisaab se random choose karo. Kabhi class 2 bhi aa sakti hai. Text generation mein variety aati hai.
import numpy as np

probs = np.array([0.659, 0.242, 0.099])
print(np.argmax(probs))                 # 0, sabse likely
choice = np.random.choice([0, 1, 2], p=probs)
print(choice)                           # probabilities ke hisaab se random

Chhoti probabilities: product se log sum tak

100 shabdon ki sentence mein probabilities multiply karoge to number bahut chhota ho jayega. Computer ke floating-point mein underflow aa sakta hai. Logs product ko sum bana dete hain:

log(p₁ × p₂ × p₃) = log p₁ + log p₂ + log p₃
  • Product: 0.8 × 0.5 × 0.25 = 0.1
  • Log sum: log 0.8 + log 0.5 + log 0.25 = log 0.1
  • Beam search aur language models logs mein score rakhte hain.

Deep learning mein ye kahan milega?

DL Course 2 ke softmax regression page mein, DL Course 5 ke language model, beam search, Word2Vec negative sampling, aur Transformer output softmax mein.

Dhyan rakhna: Probability multiply karte waqt independence assume mat karna jab relation ho. Sentence mein words independent nahi hote, isliye conditional probabilities use karte hain.
Yaad rakhna: Conditional probability context wala chance hai. Training mein correct answer ko high probability dena = likelihood badhana = cross-entropy ghatana.

Dimaag mein aaye sawaal

Cross-entropy aur negative log-likelihood same hain?

One-hot labels ke common classification setup mein practically same number milta hai: correct class ki probability ka −log.

Sampling galti kyu kare jab argmax best hai?

Creative tasks mein hamesha top word lene se text boring/repetitive ho sakta hai. Sampling controlled randomness deti hai.

Loss 2.303 ka matlab bad hai?

Depend karta hai classes pe. 10 classes mein random guess ka loss ≈ −log(0.1) = 2.303. Usse neeche jaana learning ka sign hai.

✍️ Ab tumhari baari (pehle khud socho, phir answer kholna)

50 students mein 20 ne umbrella laya. Un 20 mein 15 ko rain warning mili thi. P(warning | umbrella)?

Umbrella wale 20 mein warning wale 15. Probability = 15 ÷ 20 = 0.75.

Correct class probability 0.25 hai. Loss?

−log(0.25) ≈ 1.386.

[0.1, 0.7, 0.2] pe argmax ka index?

Sabse bada 0.7 index 1 pe hai (zero-based). Final answer 1.
