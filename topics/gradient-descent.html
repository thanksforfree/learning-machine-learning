MathsSlope, derivative aur chain rule

Samajh gaya, cost kam karna hai. Par computer best w aur b dhundhega kaise? Har value try karega?

Nahi, usme to saalon lag jayenge. Ek scene socho: raat ko tum pahad pe ho, itni dhund hai ki kuch nahi dikhta. Neeche gaon jaana hai. Kya karoge?

Pair se feel karunga ki zameen kis taraf neeche jhuk rahi hai, aur us taraf ek kadam lunga.

Exactly! Phir ruko, dobara feel karo, phir ek kadam. Baar baar. Dheere dheere neeche pahunch jaoge. Yahi gradient descent hai. Pahad = cost ki katori. Tumhari position = abhi ke w, b. Zameen ka jhukaav = gradient (slope).

Agar kadam ulte direction mein pad gaya?

Phir cost badhegi — wahi signal hai ki sign ya learning rate check karo. Training graph model ka mood-board hota hai.

w = w − α × (slope, w ke hisaab se) b = b − α × (slope, b ke hisaab se)
  • α (alpha) = learning rate = ek kadam kitna bada
  • slope (derivative) = is jagah zameen kitni aur kis taraf jhuki hai
  • minus sign = hamesha neeche ki taraf. Slope positive (aage chadhai) → w ghatega. Slope negative → w badhega. Minus khud direction sambhal leta hai.

Step-by-step numbers ke saath

Ek simple cost lo: J(w) = (w − 3)². Seedha dikh raha hai ki best w = 3 hai (tab cost 0). Iska slope = 2(w − 3). Shuru karo w = 0 se, α = 0.1 ke saath:

Kadam dekho: 0.6, 0.48, 0.38, 0.31… apne aap chhote ho rahe hain. Kyunki gaddhe ke paas zameen kam jhuki hai, slope chhota hai, to kadam bhi chhota. Jaise station paas aate hi auto wala speed dheere kar deta hai.

Learning rate — kadam ka size bahut matter karta hai

Upar wale example mein α = 1.1 lo: w = 0 → 6.6 → −1.32 → 8.18 → … har baar 3 se aur door. Isko “diverge” kehte hain. Aur α = 0.001 lo to pahunchega zaroor, par hazaaron steps mein.

Dhyan rakhna — simultaneous update: w aur b dono ka naya value purani values se calculate karo, phir dono ko ek saath badlo. Pehle w badal diya aur phir naye w se b ka slope nikala — to galat hai.

“Batch” gradient descent = har kadam pe saare training examples dekh ke slope nikaalna. Aur good news: linear regression ki katori mein sirf ek hi gaddha hota hai, galat gaddhe mein phasne ka dar nahi.

Yaad rakhna: Slope dekho → ulti taraf chhota kadam lo → repeat. Jab tak cost kam hona band na ho.

Dimaag mein aaye sawaal

Mujhe derivative / calculus nahi aata. Chalega?

Bilkul chalega. Tumhe bas itna samajhna hai: derivative = slope = kis taraf aur kitni dhalaan. Asli calculation NumPy/TensorFlow jaisi libraries kar deti hain.

Gaddhe mein pahunch ke algorithm ruk kaise jata hai?

Gaddhe ke bilkul neeche zameen flat hai → slope 0 → kadam = α × 0 = 0. Automatic ruk jata hai. Isliye α ko beech mein chhota karne ki zaroorat bhi nahi padti.

Kitne steps chalana hai?

Jab tak cost kam hona lagbhag band na ho jaye. Isko “converge hona” kehte hain. Topic 06 mein graph se check karna seekhoge.

Local minimum kya hota hai?

Agar pahad mein kai gaddhe hon, to tum sabse paas wale gaddhe mein ruk sakte ho jo sabse gehra na ho. Linear regression mein ye problem nahi (ek hi katori). Neural networks mein zameen zyada ubad-khabad hoti hai.

Achha α kaise choose karein?

Aise try karo: 0.001, 0.003, 0.01, 0.03, 0.1, 0.3… (har baar lagbhag 3 guna). Har ek ke liye cost ka graph dekho. Sabse bada α jo cost ko stable tarike se ghata raha ho — woh le lo.

✍️ Ab tumhari baari (pehle khud socho, phir answer kholna)

w = 5, slope = 4, α = 0.1 → naya w?

5 − 0.1 × 4 = 4.6.

Cost har step badh rahi hai. Pehla shak kispe?

Learning rate α bahut bada — ya code mein sign ka bug.
