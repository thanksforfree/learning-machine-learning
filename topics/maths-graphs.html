Ye kyu seekh rahe hain?Linear regression literally ek line fit karna hai, aur cost function ek katori jaisa graph hai. Graph padhna aaya to aadha Module 1 aa gaya.

Graph dekh ke samajh nahi aata kya chal raha hai.

Graph ek tasveer hai jo do cheezon ka rishta dikhati hai. Cricket ka scoreboard socho — over vs runs. Over badhe, runs badhe. Isko graph pe daalo to ek upar jaati line dikhegi. Ek nazar mein poori kahani.

Matlab graph answer nahi, kahani dikhata hai?

Haan. Graph teacher ki red pen nahi, dost ka hint hai: “dekh, yahan kuch badal raha hai”. Bas axes ko pehle padh lo, phir picture bolne lagti hai.

Do lines: x-axis aur y-axis

x-axis (neeche, left → right)Jo cheez hum dete hain (input). Jaise ghar ka size.
y-axis (left side, neeche → upar)Jo cheez hum dekhte/predict karte hain (output). Jaise price.

Point (2, 5) = x pe 2 kadam right jao, y pe 5 kadam upar. Wahan dot. Pehla number hamesha x, doosra y. Jaise address: pehle gali, phir ghar number.

Chhota sa check: agar point (4, 1) ho, to 4 right aur sirf 1 upar. Bas address follow karna hai, rocket science nahi.

Seedhi line ka formula: y = m·x + c

School wala formula, ML mein bas naam badle: y = w·x + b.

HissaNaamMatlab
w (ya m)Slopex ek kadam badhe to y kitna badhe. “Tedhapan”.
b (ya c)Interceptx = 0 pe y kitna. Line y-axis ko kahan kaat-ti hai.

Line y = 2x + 1 ko table se banao

x0123
y = 2x + 11357

Dekho: x har baar 1 badha, y har baar 2 badha → slope 2. Aur x = 0 pe y = 1 → intercept 1.

Slope ke mood

Positive slope (w > 0)Line upar chadhti hai. Bada ghar → zyada price.
Negative slope (w < 0)Line neeche girti hai. Purani gaadi → kam price.
Zero slope (w = 0)Flat line. x ka y pe koi asar nahi.
Bada slope (w = 10)Bahut tedhi line. Chhota x change → bada y change.

Curve bhi graph hi hai

Har rishta seedha nahi hota. y = x²: x = −2, −1, 0, 1, 2 → y = 4, 1, 0, 1, 4. Ye ek U-shape (katori) banata hai. Yahi katori ML mein “cost function” ki shape hogi — iska sabse neecha point dhundhna hi training hai!

Yaad rakhna: Graph = rishte ki tasveer. Line ka slope “kitna badhta hai” batata hai, intercept “kahan se shuru” batata hai.

Dimaag mein aaye sawaal

Slope graph se kaise nikalun?

Line pe do points lo. y kitna badla ÷ x kitna badla. (1, 3) se (3, 7): y 4 badha, x 2 badha → 4 ÷ 2 = 2. Isko “rise over run” bhi kehte hain — kitna chadhe ÷ kitna chale.

Axes pe negative numbers bhi hote hain?

Haan. x-axis pe 0 ke left negative, y-axis pe 0 ke neeche negative. Jaise temperature −5°C.

3 cheezein ho to graph kaise?

3D graph (jaise katori ki photo) ya “contour plot” — upar se dekha hua map jisme same height ke chhalle bane hote hain. Module 1 mein dono dekhoge.

✍️ Ab tumhari baari (pehle khud socho, phir answer kholna)

y = 3x + 2 mein x = 4 pe y? Slope aur intercept?

y = 14. Slope = 3, intercept = 2.

Points (1, 2) aur (3, 8) ke beech line ka slope?

(8 − 2) ÷ (3 − 1) = 3.
