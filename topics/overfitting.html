Model training pe achha hai, phir bhi real life mein fail kyu ho sakta hai?

Teen students socho. Rohan ne kuch khaas nahi padha, bas “har MCQ ka answer C” wala funda — practice mein bhi fail, exam mein bhi. Ye underfitting.

Priya ne concepts samjhe — practice mein achhe marks, exam mein bhi. Ye just right.

Aman ne pichle saal ka paper word-by-word ratt liya. Usi paper pe 100/100, par naya sawaal aaya to zero. Ye overfitting.

Aman wala case model mein kaisa dikhta hai?

Curve har training dot ko chhoone ke chakkar mein pagal jaisa tedha-medha ho jaata hai. Training cost ≈ 0, par naye ghar pe guess bekaar.

To training marks hi sab kuch nahi?

Bilkul nahi. Asli izzat naye question paper pe banti hai — isi ko generalization bolte hain.

NaamTechnical termPehchaan
UnderfitHigh biasTraining data pe bhi galti zyada — model ki soch bahut simple
Just rightGeneralizes wellTraining aur naye data dono pe achha
OverfitHigh varianceTraining pe almost perfect, naye data pe kharab. Data thoda badlo to model bahut badal jaaye

Overfitting ka ilaaj

Quick check: agar train error bhi high hai, pehle model ko zyada capable banao. Agar train low par dev/test high hai, overfitting wali dawai socho.

Numbers se diagnosis

Train error = jis data pe model ne seekha. Dev/test error = naye jaise data pe check. Gap kahani batata hai.

Train errorDev errorDiagnosisPehla action
28%30%UnderfitBada model / better features
2%24%OverfitZyada data / regularization
5%6%Kaafi healthyAb errors inspect karo

Bas ek number mat dekho. Train aur dev ko saath mein dekhna — jaise practice marks aur mock test dono.

1. Zyada data laaoJitne zyada examples, utna mushkil ratna. Model ko asli pattern seekhna hi padta hai.
2. Kam / better featuresBekaar features hatao (feature selection). Kam cheezein = kam ratne ka mauka.
3. RegularizationSaare features rakho, par weights ko bahut bada mat hone do — next topic.

Dimaag mein aaye sawaal

Kaise pata chalega mera model overfit hai?

Kuch data alag rakho jo training mein use nahi hua. Training pe galti kam + us alag data pe galti zyada = overfit. Module 2 mein iska poora system seekhoge.

“Bias” ka matlab yahan prejudice hai?

Nahi. Yahan technical matlab: model ki ek strong pehle-se-maani hui soch (jaise “sab kuch seedhi line hai”), jiski wajah se woh asli pattern pakad hi nahi paata.

Zyada data se underfitting bhi theek ho jayegi?

Nahi! Rohan ko 100 aur practice papers do, “sab C” wala funda wahi rahega. Underfitting ke liye model ko zyada powerful banao — zyada features, polynomial, ya neural network.

Overfitting hamesha bad thing hai?

Training pe fit hona zaroori hai, par sirf training pe fit hona problem hai. Goal hai useful pattern pakadna, random noise nahi.

Dev error kab trustworthy nahi hota?

Jab dev set bahut chhota ho ya real world se alag ho. Jaise sirf Delhi ke gharon pe dev set, par model Mumbai pe use karna hai — diagnosis mislead kar sakta hai.

✍️ Ab tumhari baari (pehle khud socho, phir answer kholna)

Train error 1%, naye data pe 25%. Kya problem?

Overfit (high variance).

Train aur naye dono pe 30%, jabki insaan 2% galti karte hain?

Underfit (high bias).
