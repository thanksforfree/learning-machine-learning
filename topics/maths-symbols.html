Main maths mein bilkul zero hoon. Formula dekhte hi dimaag band ho jaata hai.

Ek raaz batata hoon: formula sirf ek chhoti bhasha hai — jaise SMS mein “kal milte hain” ko “kl mlte h” likhte ho. Lamba sentence chhota likhne ka tareeka. Ek baar shabd samajh gaye to formula padhna kahani padhne jaisa lagega.

Achha? Example do.

Sentence: “Bill = har item ka daam × quantity, sab jodo, phir delivery charge add karo.” Formula mein: bill = daam₁ × qty₁ + daam₂ × qty₂ + delivery. Bas! Same baat, kam jagah.

To formula dekh ke panic karna allowed hai?

Bilkul allowed. Panic ko bas 10 second do, phir har symbol ko naam se bulao. Jaise naye WhatsApp group mein sab unknown numbers lagte hain — naam save hote hi scene normal.

Variable = ek dabba jisme number rakha hai

Jab hum likhte hain x = 5, matlab “x naam ke dabbe mein 5 rakha hai”. Kal usme 8 bhi rakh sakte ho — isliye “variable” (badalne wala). Letter sirf label hai: x, y, w, b koi bhi ho sakta hai.

Chhote numbers neeche aur upar — address hain, power nahi

Neeche wala: x₁, x₂, x₃“Kaunsa feature?” Jaise x₁ = size, x₂ = bedrooms. Excel ka column.
Upar bracket wala: x⁽¹⁾, x⁽²⁾“Kaunsa example?” Jaise x⁽¹⁾ = pehla ghar. Excel ki row. Bracket hai to power mat samajhna!

Σ (sigma) = “sab jodo”

Sabse daraane wala symbol, sabse aasan matlab. Σ bolta hai: “list ke har item ke liye ye karo, aur sab jod do”.

Σ xᵢ (i = 1 se 4) → x₁ + x₂ + x₃ + x₄
  • Agar x = [2, 5, 1, 4] to Σ xᵢ = 2 + 5 + 1 + 4 = 12
  • Σ xᵢ² = 4 + 25 + 1 + 16 = 46 (pehle har ek ka square, phir jodo)
  • Programmer ke liye: Σ = ek for loop jo total mein jodta jaata hai.

Formula padhne ka 3-step tareeka

1. Har symbol ka naam bolo→2. Andar se bahar padho (brackets pehle)→3. Chhote numbers daal ke try karo

Example: f = w · x + b, w = 2, x = 3, b = 1 → f = 2 · 3 + 1 = 7. Numbers daalte hi formula “dost” ban jaata hai.

Yahan ruk ke ek baar khud bolo: “w times x, plus b”. Awaaz mein bolne se formula ka darr aadha ho jaata hai, sach mein.

Yaad rakhna: Formula = chhota sentence. Symbol ka naam jaano, chhote numbers daalo, matlab khud dikh jayega.

Dimaag mein aaye sawaal

Multiply ke kai symbols kyu hain (×, ·, bina kuch)?

Sab same hain. Letters ke saath × likhne se x jaisa confuse hota hai, isliye · ya bas saath likh dete hain: 2w = 2 × w.

BODMAS yaad rakhna padega?

Basic rule: pehle brackets, phir power (²), phir × aur ÷, phir + aur −. Doubt ho to brackets laga do — computer bhi yahi rule follow karta hai.

ML mein ye sab kahan aayega?

Har jagah! Cost function mein Σ aur ², learning rate α, regularization λ, scaling mein μ aur σ, data table mein x₁⁽ⁱ⁾. Ye page tumhari dictionary hai — atko to wapas aao.

✍️ Ab tumhari baari (pehle khud socho, phir answer kholna)

x = [3, 1, 4] ho to Σ xᵢ kitna hai?

3 + 1 + 4 = 8.

x₂⁽⁵⁾ ka matlab kya hai?

5th example (row) ka 2nd feature (column).

f = w·x + b, jahan w = 4, x = 2, b = −3. f kitna?

4 × 2 − 3 = 5.
