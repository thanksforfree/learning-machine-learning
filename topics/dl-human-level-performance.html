Train error 13% hai. Ye achha hai ya kharab?

Akele 13% dekh ke jawab dena waise hi hai jaise “₹500 mehenga hai?” bina item bataye.

To context kya chahiye?

Insaan kitna achha kar sakta hai, ya best possible ke kitne paas hai. Usse pata chalta hai ki 13% shame hai ya celebration.

Human-level performance ek benchmark hai?

Haan. Especially un tasks mein jahan humans naturally good hain: photo quality check, awaaz, text moderation, handwriting.

Insaan se tulna kyu?

Human-level performance tumhe ek practical ceiling ka andaaza deta hai. Agar trained textile inspector fabric defects pe 5% galti karta hai aur tumhara model 24% pe hai, gap clear hai. Agar experienced inspector 14% galti karta hai aur model 15% pe hai, story bilkul alag hai.

Is "best possible" ko theory mein Bayes error bolte hain: woh error jo perfect classifier bhi avoid nahi kar sakta, kyunki data mein noise, blur, ambiguity hoti hai. Bayes error exact nahi milta. Human-level aksar uska useful proxy hota hai.

avoidable bias = train error − human-level error variance = dev error − train error
  • human-level error = approximate lower bound, perfect nahi par useful
  • avoidable bias = training set pe abhi kitna improve ho sakte ho
  • variance = training se dev pe jaate hi kitna toot rahe ho

Human-level ke paas progress slow kyu?

Start mein model obvious patterns seekhta hai: "kapde pe bada oil stain hai", "audio mein command clearly hai". Is stage pe improvement fast hoti hai. Human-level ke paas aate-aate bachi hui mistakes tough hoti hain: halki scratch, folded cloth shadow, confusing slang, ambiguous labels.

Jaise exam mein 40 se 80 marks lana ek type ki mehnat hai; 96 se 98 lana alag level ka microscope kaam hai.

Avoidable bias vs variance — numbers se

Do textile-quality camera projects dekhte hain. Dono ka dev error similar lag sakta hai, par dawa alag hai.

ScenarioHumanTrainDevAvoidable biasVarianceNext move
A4%15%17%15 − 4 = 11%17 − 15 = 2%Bias: bigger model/features
B11%12%22%12 − 11 = 1%22 − 12 = 10%Variance: more data/regularize

A mein model training set pe hi human se door hai. B mein train human ke paas hai, par dev pe toot raha hai. Same train number ka matlab context ke saath badal gaya.

Human-level kise maanein?

Textile inspection task mein "insaan" kaun? Naya trainee? Shift ka experienced QC person? Do inspectors ka audit? Choice matter karti hai.

BenchmarkErrorUse kab
Naya trainee18%Training app UX compare karna
Experienced QC9%Factory floor target
2-inspector audit5%Best practical proxy for Bayes error

Usually, jis performance ko product beat karna chahta hai, usko benchmark banao. Agar tool QC worker ko assist karega, experienced QC relevant hai. Agar audit team final arbiter hai, audit-level useful hai.

Human-level se aage nikalna

Kuch kaam mein model humans se aage nikal sakta hai: ad click prediction, recommender ranking, fraud patterns, huge table data. Kyu? Kyunki humans ke paas itna raw data dekhne ki capacity nahi.

Par photo/audio/text jaise natural perception tasks mein human-level ke aas-paas aana common hai; usse aage jaana harder hota hai. Hard ka matlab impossible nahi. Bas diagnosis mein human benchmark ka role dheere-dheere kam hota jaata hai.

Model sudhaarne ki checklist

Numbers dekh ke next action chuno. Ye checklist fridge pe chipka sakte ho.

High avoidable biasBigger network, train longer, architecture change, input features better, optimization debug.
High varianceMore data, augmentation, L2/dropout, simpler model, data leakage check.
Near human-levelError analysis karo. Ambiguous examples, label quality, rare cases, product metric check.
Beyond human-levelHuman benchmark less useful. Dev/test and business metrics pe rely karo.
Dhyan rakhna: Human-level ko blindly “Bayes error” mat maan lo. Humans bhi tired, biased, ya untrained ho sakte hain. Benchmark ka source likh ke rakho.
Yaad rakhna: Error number tab bolta hai jab tum uske paas baseline rakhte ho. Warna 13% sirf hawa mein latakta number hai.

Dimaag mein aaye sawaal

Agar task mein humans kharab hain to?

Tab human-level weak benchmark hai. Jaise millions of transactions mein fraud pattern. Wahan dev/test metric, business cost, aur historical system comparison zyada useful.

Human-level measure karna expensive hai. Skip kar sakte hain?

Small sample pe rough audit karo. 100–200 examples bhi direction de sakte hain. Perfect number ka wait karte karte project mat rok do.

Model human se better hai, phir bhi mistakes scary hain. Kya karein?

Safety-critical task mein average better hona kaafi nahi. Worst-case, subgroups, calibration, monitoring, human review process — sab design karo.

✍️ Ab tumhari baari (pehle khud socho, phir answer kholna)

Human 5%, train 18%, dev 20%. Bias aur variance?

Avoidable bias = 18 − 5 = 13%. Variance = 20 − 18 = 2%. Final answer: high avoidable bias.

Human 12%, train 13%, dev 25%. Next move?

Avoidable bias = 1%, variance = 12%. Train human ke paas, dev bahut kharab. More data/regularization. Final answer: high variance.

Trainee 18%, experienced QC 9%, audit pair 5%, model train 8%, dev 11%. Human-level kis number se compare karoge?

Agar best practical lower bound chahiye to audit pair 5%. Factory workflow compare ke liye 9% bhi useful. Final answer: context ke hisaab se, diagnosis mein 5% stronger proxy.
