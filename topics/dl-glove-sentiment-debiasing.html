Embeddings pehle se mil gaye. Ab inka use kya? Sirf “king − man + woman” wali party trick?

Nahi dost, asli kaam yahin se shuru hota hai. Embedding ek shabd ka chhota sa meaning-address hai. Is address se sentiment, translation, search, sab mein help milti hai.

Par ye address seekhta kaise hai?

Ek tareeka hai: kaunse shabd saath-saath aate hain, uski ginti karo. “Chai” ke paas “doodh” aur “adrak” zyada milenge; “platform” ke paas “train”. Isi se GloVe embeddings seekhta hai.

GloVe ka seedha idea: saath dikhne wali ginti

Socho tumne bahut saare WhatsApp food reviews aur news articles padhe. Har baar jab word i ke aas-paas word j dikha, tum ek counter badha dete ho. Is counter ko Xij bolte hain: co-occurrence count.

GloVe ka mood simple hai: agar do words baar-baar saath aaye, unke vectors ka dot product unki log-count ke paas hona chahiye. Bahut rare pair ko model over-serious na le, isliye ek weight function bhi hota hai.

J = Σ f(Xij) × (θᵢᵀ eⱼ + bᵢ + b'ⱼ − log Xij)²
  • Xij = word i aur word j kitni baar aas-paas aaye
  • eⱼ aur θᵢ = do learned vectors; training ke baad inhi se embedding milti hai
  • bᵢ, b'ⱼ = bias terms, jaise har word ka personal adjustment
  • f(Xij) = weight; bahut chhoti/badi counts ko balance karta hai

Yahan sum sirf un word-pairs par hota hai jinka count Xij positive hai. Jo pair kabhi saath nahi aaya, uska log-count define nahi hota; use objective mein seedha daalte hi nahi.

chai, doodh403.693.60(3.60 − 3.69)² = 0.008

Yahan numbers bas tiny demo hain. Real training mein vocabulary hazaaron words ki hoti hai, aur table bahut badi. Par intuition wahi: words ke neighbourhood se meaning nikalna.

Axes ka matlab hamesha human-readable nahi hota

Embedding vector ke 300 numbers dekh ke bolna tempting hai: “dimension 17 = royalness, dimension 92 = foodness.” Kabhi-kabhi rough pattern mil sakta hai, par generally axes rotate ho sakti hain. Meaning poore space ke relations mein chhupa hota hai, single column mein nahi.

Friendly warning: Embedding ko Excel sheet ki columns jaisa mat sochna. Zyada useful sawaal hai: “kaun kiske paas hai?” aur “kis direction mein difference ja raha hai?”

Sentiment classification: average se shuru, RNN se sudhaar

Review: “khana mast tha”. Har word ki embedding nikalo, average lo, phir softmax/sigmoid se positive ya negative predict karo. Chhote project ke liye ye surprisingly kaam kar sakta hai.

WordMini embedding [taste, complaint]
mast[0.80, 0.10]
slow[−0.60, 0.90]
Average of “mast slow”[(0.80 − 0.60)/2, (0.10 + 0.90)/2] = [0.10, 0.50]

Par average order bhool jaata hai. “not good” mein good positive hai, average model confuse ho sakta hai. “good, not bad” aur “not good, bad service” jaise sentences mein order, negation, aur context matter karta hai.

Isliye sentiment ke liye RNN many-to-one better hota hai: words ek-ek karke padho, hidden state update karo, last mein sentiment do. RNN “not” ko next word pe effect karne ka chance deta hai.

import numpy as np
emb = {"not": np.array([-0.2, 0.8]), "good": np.array([0.9, 0.1])}
x = (emb["not"] + emb["good"]) / 2   # average model order bhool gaya
score = np.dot(x, np.array([1.0, -0.6]))
print(score)  # RNN hota to "not" ka context yaad rakh sakta

Bias: embeddings samaj ka mirror bhi ban sakte hain

Embeddings internet/text se seekhte hain. Agar data mein “doctor” baar-baar male naam ke paas aur “nurse” female naam ke paas dikha, vector space bhi woh pattern utha lega. Model phir resume screening, search, ya autocomplete mein unfair behaviour kar sakta hai.

Bias direction
gender jaise axis dhundo→Neutralize
neutral words se component hatao→Equalize
paired words ko fair distance do
  • Direction pehchano: jaise ehe − eshe, eman − ewoman ka average ek gender direction de sakta hai.
  • Neutralize: “doctor”, “engineer” jaise words gender-neutral hone chahiye. Unke vector se gender direction wala part hata do.
  • Equalize: “grandmother” aur “grandfather” jaise pair genuinely gendered hain, par kisi neutral word ke relation mein unfair tilt nahi hona chahiye.
Dhyan rakhna: Debiasing ek magic eraser nahi. Data, labels, product design, evaluation — sab check karna padta hai. Sirf embedding adjust karke fairness ka certificate nahi milta.
Yaad rakhna: GloVe co-occurrence ko fit karke embeddings banata hai; embeddings useful bhi hain, aur responsibility bhi laati hain.

Dimaag mein aaye sawaal

GloVe aur Word2Vec mein kaunsa “best” hai?

Universal winner nahi. Word2Vec local prediction task se seekhta hai; GloVe global co-counts fit karta hai. Dono useful rahe hain. Aaj kal bade language models contextual embeddings dete hain, par basic intuition yahi hai.

Average embeddings kab use karna theek hai?

Jab baseline chahiye, data kam hai, aur sentence simple hai. Production ya tricky language mein RNN/attention/transformer better choice hota hai.

Bias direction manually choose karna risky nahi?

Haan, isliye carefully define karo, multiple word pairs lo, aur downstream tests karo. Fairness mein “measure, fix, re-measure” wala discipline zaroori hai.

✍️ Ab tumhari baari (pehle khud socho, phir answer kholna)

Agar Xij = 100 aur model score 4.50 hai, log Xij ≈ 4.61. f(X) = 1 maan ke squared error kya?

Error = (4.50 − 4.61)² = (−0.11)² = 0.0121.

“not tasty” ko average embedding model kyu galat padh sakta hai?

Kyunki average order aur negation ka role almost mita deta hai. “tasty” ka positive signal bach jaata hai, “not” ka effect next word pe properly apply nahi hota.

Neutralize step “king” pe lagana chahiye?

Nahi. “king/queen” gendered pair hai. Neutralize “pilot”, “teacher”, “coder” jaise profession words pe zyada sensible hai.
