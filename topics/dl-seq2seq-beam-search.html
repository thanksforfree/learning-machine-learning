Hindi sentence ko English mein translate karna hai. Ek RNN left se right padh lega, phir English likh dega?

Bilkul, pehla classic setup wahi tha: encoder-decoder. Encoder input sentence ko digest karta hai. Decoder us digest se output sentence banata hai.

Par output word choose kaise kare? Har step pe top word?

Wahi greedy temptation hai. Jaise buffet mein pehli plate mein sirf gulab jamun bhar liya, baad mein dal-chawal ki jagah nahi bachi. Translation mein local best word final sentence ko kharab kar sakta hai.

Encoder-decoder: ek sentence andar, dusra bahar

Input x = “mujhe bhookh lag rahi hai”. Encoder RNN har Hindi word padhta hai aur hidden state update karta hai. Last state mein sentence ka compressed summary aata hai. Decoder us summary ko initial state ki tarah use karke English words generate karta hai: “I feel hungry”.

Image captioning bhi seq2seq jaisa hi

Image mein sequence input nahi hota, par output sentence hota hai. CNN image se feature vector nikalta hai, phir RNN decoder caption banata hai: “ek ladka cricket khel raha hai”. Matlab encoder alag type ka ho sakta hai, decoder language model jaisa hi chal sakta hai.

Conditional language model: P(y | x)

Normal language model P(y) bolta hai: “ye English sentence kitna natural hai?” Translation model bolta hai: P(y | x) — “input Hindi x diya hai, ye English y kitna likely hai?”

P(y | x) = P(y₁ | x) × P(y₂ | y₁, x) × P(y₃ | y₁, y₂, x) × ...
  • x = source sentence, jaise “mujhe bhookh lag rahi hai”
  • y₁, y₂... = output words, jaise “I”, “feel”, “hungry”
  • Decoder har step pe pichhle output aur encoder summary dono dekhta hai.

Greedy kyu galat ho sakta hai

Greedy har step pe sabse bada current probability word uthata hai. Dekho tiny example: input ka best translation “I feel hungry” hai, par second step pe “am” thoda zyada tempting lag raha hai.

DecisionChoiceLocal probabilityTotal so far
word 1I0.5460.546
word 2 after Iam0.4000.546 × 0.400 = 0.218
word 3 after I amhungry0.3000.218 × 0.300 = 0.065
word 2 after Ifeel0.3500.546 × 0.350 = 0.191
word 3 after I feelhungry0.9000.191 × 0.900 = 0.172

Greedy ne “am” pick kiya kyunki 0.400 > 0.350. Par poora sentence dekho: 0.172 zyada hai. Translation mein shaadi ka menu jaisa hai, ek dish alone best nahi, poori plate best honi chahiye.

Beam search: top B raaste saath le chalo

Beam search ek hi raasta nahi pakadta. Har step pe top B partial sentences rakhta hai. B = 3 matlab teen best candidates pocket mein. Har candidate ko expand karo, total probability nikalo, phir top 3 bachao.

StepCandidates consideredTop 3 kept
1I 0.546, me 0.331, we 0.082, hungry 0.041I, me, we
2I am 0.218, I feel 0.191, me hungry 0.149, me feel 0.116, I want 0.055I am, I feel, me hungry
3I am hungry 0.065, I feel hungry 0.172, me hungry ⟨EOS⟩ 0.089I feel hungry wins

Bas! Beam ne “I feel” ko zinda rakha, isliye final better sentence mila. Agar B bahut chhota hai, good path jaldi mar sakta hai. Agar B bahut bada hai, compute mehenga ho jaata hai.

score(y) = Π⟨t⟩ P(y⟨t⟩ | y⟨1⟩...y⟨t−1⟩, x)
  • Π = probabilities multiply karna
  • y⟨t⟩ = output ka t-th word
  • x = input sentence

B = 1 matlab greedy

Agar beam width B = 1 rakho, har step pe sirf ek candidate bachega. Ye exactly greedy decoding ban jaata hai. B = 3, 10, 100 jaise values search ko broader banati hain, par guarantee abhi bhi nahi milti ki global best pakka mil gaya.

beam = [("", 1.0)]
for step in range(max_len):
    new_beam = []
    for sent, prob in beam:
        for word, p in next_word_probs(sent):
            new_beam.append((sent + " " + word, prob * p))
    beam = sorted(new_beam, key=lambda x: x[1], reverse=True)[:3]  # B = 3
Dhyan rakhna: Beam search training nahi hai. Ye sirf prediction time pe output choose karne ka search method hai. Model ki probabilities kharab hain to beam bhi confidence ke saath galat sentence de sakta hai.
Yaad rakhna: Seq2seq P(y | x) seekhta hai; beam search top B partial sentences ko bachake greedy ki jaldbazi kam karta hai.

Dimaag mein aaye sawaal

Encoder ka last state poora sentence ka meaning kaise yaad rakhega?

Short sentences mein theek-thaak. Long sentences mein mushkil hoti hai, isliye attention aaya. Attention decoder ko input ke alag-alag parts dobara dekhne deta hai.

Beam search mein probability multiply karte karte bahut chhoti nahi ho jaati?

Haan. Practical code mein logs use karte hain. Product ki jagah log probabilities ka sum. Agle topic mein ye clean tarike se dekhenge.

B bada karne se output hamesha better?

Mostly search better hota hai, par hamesha nahi. Kabhi model short ya weird sentence ko zyada probability deta hai. Isliye length normalization aur BLEU jaise tools kaam aate hain.

✍️ Ab tumhari baari (pehle khud socho, phir answer kholna)

Candidate A probability: 0.5 × 0.4 × 0.8. Candidate B: 0.4 × 0.7 × 0.7. Kaun better?

A = 0.160. B = 0.196. Candidate B better, bhale first word lower tha.

Image captioning mein encoder aur decoder ka role kya?

CNN encoder image ko feature vector mein badalta hai. RNN decoder us vector se caption ke words generate karta hai.

Agar B = 1 hai, beam search ko kya bol sakte?

Greedy search. Sirf ek partial sentence bacha, to alternate raaste mar gaye.
