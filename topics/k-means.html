Computer groups dhundhta kaise hai? Koi magic wand hai?

Magic nahi, ek desi khel. Bade maidan mein log khade hain. Tum K = 2 jhande random jagah gaad dete ho. Phir bolte ho: “har insaan apne sabse paas wale jhande ke paas chala jaye.” Do teams ban gayi.

Ab har jhanda uthao aur apni team ke beech (average jagah) gaad do. Jhande khisak gaye! To log dobara check karein: ab kaunsa jhanda paas hai? Kuch log team badlenge. Phir jhande dobara beech mein. Baar baar — jab tak koi team na badle. Yahi K-means hai.

1. K centers random rakho→2. Har point sabse paas wale center ko→3. Center ko apne group ke average pe le jao↻Koi badlav nahi → ruk jao

Center ko centroid kehte hain — group ka “center of gravity”. Naam heavy hai, par matlab bas group ka average point.

Chhota sa example — ek line pe 6 points

Customers ke monthly visits: 1, 2, 3, 10, 11, 12. K = 2. Shuruaati centers galat jagah: c₁ = 1, c₂ = 3.

Galat shuruaat ke baawajood do round mein sensible groups mil gaye: “kabhi-kabhi aane wale” (1–3) aur “regular” (10–12). Yahan ruk ke table khud check kar lo — 3 sach mein 1.5 se zyada paas hai ya 9 se?

“Paas” kaise naapte hain?

Seedhi doori (distance) — map pe do jagah ke beech ki line. Do features ho to Pythagoras wala tareeka: √((x₁ − c₁)² + (x₂ − c₂)²). Code mein np.linalg.norm(x - c). Bas yaad rakho: “paas” ka matlab numbers se decide hota hai, aankhon se nahi.

Dhyan rakhna: Agar ek feature “salary” (lakhs) aur doosra “rating” (1–5) hai to doori mein salary hi hawi rahegi. Clustering se pehle feature scaling karo.

2D distance ka ek round

Line wala example easy tha. Do features mein assignment kaise hota hai?

Same idea. Har point se har center ki distance nikaalo. Jo kam, point us team mein. Bas Pythagoras ka calculator version.

Centers: c₁ = (0,0), c₂ = (4,0). Teen points assign karte hain.

A (1,1)√(1²+1²)=1.41√(3²+1²)=3.16c₁

Agar c₁ ko A mila aur c₂ ko B mila, naye centers A aur B ke average pe jayenge. Agar multiple points mile, unka average. K-means ka “means” wahi average hai.

Dhyan rakhna: Tie rare hota hai. Code mein koi fixed rule hota hai, jaise pehla center choose karna. Isse usually final result pe bada farak nahi padta.

Center move ka average check

Assignment ke baad centroid simply apni team ka average hota hai. Do points A(1,1) aur D(3,3) c₁ team mein aaye:

CoordinateAverageNew center
x(1+3)/2 = 2(2,2)
y(1+3)/2 = 2

Isi average ki wajah se center team ke beech mein aa jaata hai. Phir assignments dobara check hote hain.

Beginner checkpoint: K-means mein labels ka naam algorithm nahi jaanta. Center 1 aur center 2 bas IDs hain; meaning tum data dekh ke doge.

Distance formula features ke units pe sensitive hai. Height meters mein aur income rupees mein ho to income wali direction center ko kheench legi. Isliye scaling K-means ka “pehle brush karo” step samjho.

Yaad rakhna: K-means = “paas wale center ko jao” + “center ko group ke beech le jao” — repeat.

Dimaag mein aaye sawaal

Koi center ke paas ek bhi point na aaye to?

Aam taur pe us center ko hata dete hain (K − 1 groups), ya use random nayi jagah rakh ke dobara try karte hain.

Kya K-means hamesha ruk jaata hai?

Haan. Har step pe total doori ya to ghatti hai ya same rehti hai — agle topic mein iska “cost” dekhenge. Isliye ye hamesha kahin na kahin settle ho jaata hai.

Groups saaf-saaf alag na hon to?

Tab bhi K-means groups bana dega. Jaise T-shirt sizes: logon ki height-weight ek lagatar phailaav hai, par K = 3 se S/M/L bana sakte ho. Useful hai, chahe natural border na ho.

K-means round kitni baar chalega?

Jab assignments change hona band ho jayein, ya centers almost move na karein, ya max iterations hit ho jaayein. Practical libraries max_iter rakhti hain.

✍️ Ab tumhari baari (pehle khud socho, phir answer kholna)

Points 0, 1, 9, 10; centers 0 aur 1. Pehle round ke baad naye centers?

c₁ = {0} → 0, c₂ = {1, 9, 10} → 6.67. Agle round mein 1 ab 0 ke paas → c₁ = 0.5, c₂ = 9.5.

K-means se pehle scaling kyu?

Bade unit wala feature doori pe hawi ho jaata hai.
